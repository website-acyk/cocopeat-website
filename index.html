Why Cocopeat

A Better Growing Medium, By Nature

Cocopeat starts as a coconut husk — a byproduct of the coconut industry that would otherwise be discarded or burned.

Natural & Renewable

Made from coconut husk — a renewable resource that comes naturally from the coconut industry, not a mined or synthetic material.

Reduces Waste

Turns discarded coconut husk into a useful growing medium, instead of letting it go to landfill or open burning.

Biodegradable

A natural material that decomposes after use, returning organic matter to the soil rather than persisting as waste.

Water Management

Retains water effectively when used appropriately, which can help reduce how much water plants need.

Low Environmental Impact

Reuses agricultural waste and biodegrades naturally, giving it a relatively light footprint compared with synthetic media.

Versatile

Suitable across a range of agricultural and horticultural growing applications, from hobby pots to commercial operations.

FAQ

Common Questions

Cocopeat is a natural growing medium made from coconut husk — a byproduct of the coconut industry. It's biodegradable, renewable, and used as a soil amendment or growing medium in agriculture and horticulture.

Our Cocopeat Sack is used for direct potting and home gardening. Our Cocopeat Pallet is used in freight, export and supply-chain logistics as a wood-free alternative to timber pallets.

They're very different products. The Cocopeat Sack is loose, uncompressed cocopeat, ready to use straight from the bag for growing. The Cocopeat Pallet is a rigid, molded pallet made from upcycled agricultural waste, used to replace timber pallets in shipping and logistics.

In our own testing, shown above, the Cocopeat Pallet held up to repeated impact by hand alongside a plastic pallet and a timber pallet — both of which were damaged. It's also exempt from ISPM 15 fumigation treatment, since it isn't made of wood.
